## Supported editions

*Supported editions for this feature: Frontline Starter, Frontline Standard, and Frontline Plus; Business Starter, Business Standard, and Business Plus; Enterprise Standard and Enterprise Plus; Education Fundamentals, Education Standard, and Education Plus; Nonprofits; G Suite Basic and G Suite Business.* [Compare your edition](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/getting-started/editions/compare-google-workspace-editions)

## Overview

As an administrator, you can add your organization's logo to some of your Google Workspace service pages, such as Gmail, Calendar, and Drive. The logo appears at the top of each user's window for all domains added to your account.

*Screenshot: the top-right toolbar of a Google Workspace page (support, help, settings and apps-grid icons), with a sample custom logo shown in a highlighted box next to the user's profile picture.*

## Logo image requirements

- The logo must be either a JPEG, PNG, or GIF format. **Note**: GIF images are not animated
- The logo on your services pages is displayed at exactly 320 x 132 pixels; therefore, the recommended image size that you upload is exactly 320 x 132 pixels.

## Before you begin

- You can upload your own logo for your Google Workspace account. All organizational units and domains in that account use that logo.
- If you use the Safari browser, you can't upload your organization's logo. To upload the logo, you must use the Google Chrome browser.
- The logo is only visible to users within your organization.
- If you use a mobile device, the logo won't be visible. If you want to see the logo, use your computer instead.

## Upload your logo

1. In the Google Admin console, go to Menu > Account > Account settings > Personalization.

   [Go to Personalization](https://admin.google.com/ac/companyprofile/personalization)

   Requires having the [Domain settings](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/users/administrator-privilege-definitions#domains) administrator privilege.
2. To open the **Personalization** panel, click the Down arrow.
3. Click **Select file to upload** and select the logo image on your computer. You'll see a preview your logo.
4. Click **Save**. It might take **up to 4 days** for your new logo to appear on your Google Workspace pages.

## Logo policies

When you create your logo, we encourage you to be creative. However, to stay within our [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms), keep the following points in mind:

- You can use the term "Powered by Google" anywhere on your page and in your logo.
- Except for "Powered by Google," your logo can't contain the words "Google," "Gmail," or any of Google's other trademarked terms.
- Avoid using the Google logo, the Gmail logo, or any other Google logos in your custom logo.
- Illegal or copyrighted images are prohibited.
